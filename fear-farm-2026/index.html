Driving times are approximate, not live traffic. Fear Farm tickets are date-specific and must be purchased online before arrival. Prices are CAD.

Entry prices

  • Fear Farm: $64.99; approximately $78.52 including taxes/fees.
  • Brantview: free orchard entry; apple bags/food extra.
  • Pinehurst Lake: $9.50/adult or $19/vehicle via ePass (gate unstaffed, up to 6).
  • Snyder’s early access: $8 add-on with Fear Farm ticket.

Before you go

  • Pinehurst Lake: road-construction detours may affect access.
  • Brantview: check October apple-picking varieties and wagon availability.
  • Weather: layers, comfortable shoes and a jacket.
  • Timing: allow extra time for parking, dinner and queues.
