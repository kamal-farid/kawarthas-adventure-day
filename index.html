Burleigh Falls → Warsaw Caves → Selwyn Beach
Rapids, caves, hiking, a picnic and a beach finish — all in one day.

The plan

Adventure first. Beach finish.

Leave the GTA at 9:00 AM, meet at Burleigh Falls, spend the main part of the afternoon at Warsaw Caves, then finish at Selwyn Beach.

9:00 AM departure works, but it makes the day tighter.
The caves stay intact; the optional longer hike is shortened so you can leave Warsaw before its 4:30 PM gatehouse close.

Day itinerary

Revised schedule

~11:15 AM–12:00 PM

Burleigh Falls

Meet up, walk the falls and Lock 28 area, take photos and use the washrooms. Do not swim in the rapids.

12:00–12:30 PM

Drive to Warsaw Caves

Short transfer south. Park, gear up and head straight for the caves.

12:30–2:30 PM

Caves + Lookout Trail

Explore Warsaw’s seven limestone caves and the surrounding rock formations. Bring a headlamp and shoes with grip.

2:30–3:10 PM

Picnic by the Indian River

Eat at the picnic area near the beach and take a short break before the final Warsaw activity.

3:10–4:05 PM

Short hike + beach cooldown

Do a shorter section of Warsaw’s trail network, then cool down near the Indian River. This replaces the earlier 1.5-hour hike.

4:05–4:20 PM

Pack up and leave Warsaw

Give yourselves a buffer before the 4:30 PM gatehouse close.

~4:45–7:00 PM

Selwyn Beach

Swim if conditions are good, relax, throw a football or frisbee and finish the day by Chemong Lake.

~7:00 PM

Head back to the GTA

Expect an evening arrival depending on traffic and which side of the GTA you’re returning to.
